A little about me

I like the work
behind the interface.

My work has spanned restaurant operations, contact-center tools, connected devices, and independent game design. I’m interested in the decisions that make complex products understandable: what people need to see, what the system can support, and how to keep work moving when something is incomplete.

These cases bring together original product screens, recorded interactions, and the thinking behind the work. Presentations live in their own section, for a more guided walkthrough.
