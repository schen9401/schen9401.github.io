Review with product & engineering

We could enter by payment.
We could not close by payment.

The blocked assumption

A completed payment could be treated as an independently closable item.

The system could close only the whole check, once every payment had the required information.

PM and engineering review ruled out the payment-level closing direction. The design needed a compatible model for progress.

Source: presenter's account of the constraint and PM/engineering review.

2 minutes. This is the central decision in the case. Say that engineering and PM review exposed the limitation; do not claim I independently discovered it or persuaded executives to change the architecture. Explain why the version was blocked and how that narrowed the solution space.
